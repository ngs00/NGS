I am a Senior Researcher at the Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology (KRICT) while pursuing a Ph.D. in Data Science at KAIST under the supervision of Prof. [Chanyoung Park](https://dsail.kaist.ac.kr/professor/). My research mainly focuses on AI-driven mathematical modeling of complex physical and chemical systems for molecular generation, materials discovery, and autonomous laboratory.

My research interests span mathematical optimization, AI for science, and AI-induced scientific reasoning, with a particular focus on physics- and chemistry-informed modeling, governing equation discovery, and inverse design.
